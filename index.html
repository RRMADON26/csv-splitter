Frequently Asked Questions

Is CSV Toolkit free?

Yes. All six tools are free, with no sign-up and no limit on how many files you process.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Files are read and processed by your browser on your own device. They are never sent to a server, which is why the tools also work with confidential data.

How large can a CSV file be?

Files with hundreds of thousands of rows are handled comfortably, and much larger ones work too. Files are read in chunks, so the practical limit is your device’s memory. In Chrome and Edge you can save results straight into a folder, which avoids holding them in memory.

Does it work with Excel?

Yes. Convert creates real .xlsx workbooks, and any CSV can be saved as UTF-8 with BOM so Excel shows accented characters correctly.

Which delimiters and encodings are supported?

Comma, semicolon, tab and pipe delimiters, and UTF-8, UTF-16 and Windows-1252 encodings. Both are detected automatically and can be changed by hand.

Does it work on a phone or tablet?

Yes. The layout adapts to small screens and every tool works on mobile browsers.

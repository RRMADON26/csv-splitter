Privacy policy

Last updated October 17, 2026

The short version: the files you open in CSV Toolkit are processed on your device and are never uploaded. This page explains that in detail, along with the few other kinds of data involved in visiting the site.

Your files

When you choose or drop a file, it is read by JavaScript running in your browser. Splitting, merging, inspecting, transforming and converting all happen on your own device, and the results are given to you as downloads or saved to a folder you pick. Your files and their contents are not sent to us or to anyone else, and we have no way to see them.

Data stored in your browser

If you save settings as a preset, the preset is stored in your browser’s local storage under the name csvkit.presets.v1. It stays on your device and is not transmitted. You can remove it at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.

Server logs

Like any website, the host that delivers these pages may keep standard access logs, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and to keep the service running. These logs never include the contents of your files, because your files are never sent to the server.

Advertising and cookies

This site may show ads served by Google AdSense. When it does:

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google’s My Ad Center. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.
  • Google explains how it uses information from sites that show its ads in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, through a consent message provided by Google. You can change your choice later from the link in that message.

Ad code runs separately from the tools and never receives your files.

Analytics

CSV Toolkit does not use analytics or tracking scripts of its own.

Children

The site is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent through the contact page.
